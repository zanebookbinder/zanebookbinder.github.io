ShelfAware Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the ShelfAware iOS app ("ShelfAware," "we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use it. ShelfAware lets you photograph a bookshelf and identifies the books on it. We built the app to collect as little as possible: there are no accounts, no logins, and your scan history is stored only on your device.

Information We Collect

ShelfAware collects only what it needs to identify books:

  • Photos you provide. When you start a scan, ShelfAware uses the bookshelf photo you take with your camera or choose from your photo library. The app accesses your camera or photo library only for this purpose, and only after you grant iOS permission.
  • An anonymous device identifier. A random identifier is generated when you install the app. It is not linked to your name, Apple ID, or any personal account, and is used only to apply fair-use limits and prevent abuse of the service.
  • Basic technical request data. When the app contacts our server, standard information such as your IP address is processed transiently to route the request and protect against abuse.

We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, precise location, or advertising identifiers, and we do not require you to create an account.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information above solely to:

  • Identify the books in the photo you submit and return their titles, cover art, ratings, summaries, and shelf positions;
  • Apply per-device usage limits and protect the service from abuse; and
  • Diagnose errors and keep the app working reliably.

We do not use your photos or data for advertising, and we do not sell your personal information.

How Your Data Is Shared — Third-Party AI & Service Providers

ShelfAware sends the photo you submit to a third-party AI service to identify the books in it. Before your first scan, the app asks for your permission and explains this; no photo is sent unless you agree.

To provide the app's core feature, we share data with the following processors:

  • Anthropic (Claude AI), via Amazon Web Services (AWS Bedrock). The bookshelf photo you submit is transmitted to Anthropic's Claude model, accessed through our secure backend on Amazon Web Services, to recognize the books on the shelf. The photo is processed to generate the result and is not used to train AI models, is not linked to your identity, and is not retained after the identification is complete.
  • Amazon Web Services (AWS). Our backend runs on AWS, which processes requests and briefly caches non-personal book metadata on our behalf.
  • Book metadata providers (Google Books, Open Library, Hardcover). To enrich results, our backend sends book titles and authors (not your photo and not personal information) to these services to retrieve details such as ratings, descriptions, and genres.

These providers act as our service providers/processors and are contractually bound to protect the data with the same or equivalent protections described in this policy, and to use it only to perform their services for us. We do not otherwise share, rent, or sell your data.

Data Retention

  • Photos: processed transiently to identify books and not stored on our servers afterward.
  • Anonymous device identifier & usage counters: kept only as long as needed to enforce fair-use limits (rolling short-term counters).
  • Cached book metadata (not personal): book details may be cached on our servers for up to about 100 days to speed up future lookups.
  • On-device data: your scans, saved books, and history live only on your device. Deleting the app removes them permanently.

Your Choices and Rights

  • Consent: the app requests your permission before sending any photo to the AI service. You can decline, in which case no photo is sent and no scan is performed.
  • Permissions: you control camera and photo-library access at any time in the iOS Settings app.
  • Deletion: because your library is stored on-device, deleting the app deletes your data. For any questions about data handled by our backend, contact us using the details below.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA (to access, correct, or delete personal information). We honor these requests where they apply.

Children's Privacy

ShelfAware is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Requests between the app and our backend are encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and signed to prevent tampering. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above and post the new version on this page. Material changes will be reflected here before they take effect.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, contact us at zane.bookbinder@gmail.com.
